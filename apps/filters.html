Compact filters

BIP 158: each block compresses to a ~2% Golomb-coded digest of its scripts. This app fetches a window of at most 6 raw blocks, builds their filters with the schema engine (byte-identical to the official vectors), and tests your address against them — the query never leaves your browser. A match means "fetch this block and look"; that is the whole light-client privacy model. · schema · spv · blocks
